### 19. SteerDistill: Quantizing LLM Agents via Closed-Loop Teacher Steering
- Time: 2026-11-12
- Category: Models & Research
- Summary: SteerDistill addresses the drop in task success when LLM agents are quantized to low bit-widths, where agents overlook task requirements or environment conditions and diverge from full-precision behavior. The method uses a full-precision teacher to guide a student through failed rollouts, refines guidance with student execution feedback, and trains the student to internalize corrections for unassisted inference. Across five agentic benchmarks and three models, it beats the strongest quantized baseline by 1.67–2.18 average points under W4A16 and stays within one point of BF16; code is available at the linked GitHub repository.
- Comment: The closed-loop teacher-steering approach may help make low-bit agent deployment more reliable, though gains are measured on a limited benchmark set.
- Code: https://github.com/air-embodied-brain/SteerDistill
