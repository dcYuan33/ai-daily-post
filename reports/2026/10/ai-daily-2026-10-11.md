### 9. Hugging Face releases JEV-27B-VL: a multi-modal decision-making model that does not rely on behavioral data and only looks at the cover to make recommendations
- Time: 2026-10-11
- Category: Models & Research
- Summary: The Hugging Face blog introduces JEV-27B-VL released by autotrust, which is a visual version of JEV-27B: after inputting images and text, the model gives a calibration probability for each candidate option in a forward propagation. When multi-step reasoning is required, it can also think step by step while looking at the picture. The author claims that the decision head has never seen images when training. On the MicroLens public short video data set, the "next watch" effect based on the video cover alone is comparable to collaborative filtering based on the viewing history of 59,045 users (AUC 0.727 vs. 0.728, Top-5 hit rate 59% vs. 49%); text capabilities remain unchanged, and indicators such as RewriteBench 89.9 are given. The model is released with Apache-2.0 open weight.
- Comment: Making the "decision" a forward score instead of generating text may be a way to reduce the cost of agent routing and content review. However, the comparative figures are all from the publisher's self-report, and it is worth paying attention to subsequent third-party recurrences.
- Sources: [Hugging Face Blog (A)](https://huggingface.co/blog/autotrust/autotrustjev-27b-vl-a-decision-model-that-learned)
