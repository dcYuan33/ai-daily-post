### 29. FewStepRL: Fine-tuning Few-step Diffusion Models with Reinforcement Learning
- Time: 2026-11-10
- Category: Models & Research
- Summary: Reinforcement learning for diffusion models is expensive because many-step samplers require repeated rollouts, while few-step models predict an average velocity over an interval rather than the instantaneous velocity assumed by existing objectives. FewStepRL adapts RL objectives to few-step models through a transformation to x-space, a gradient decomposition into self-regression and reward augmentation, and an adaptation that computes anchors from the few-step model. This yields few-step versions of FlowGRPO, AWM, DiffusionNFT, DiffusionOPSD, and ReFL in one modular codebase; reward-gradient methods work best when gradients are affordable, and DiffusionNFT-style methods otherwise reach high rewards in relatively few iterations, with code available.
- Code: https://github.com/EDAPINENUT/fewsteprl-sd35
- Sources: [Papers With Code](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.08240)

### 30. TRIO: Task Refinement with Individual Optimization for Retrieval-Augmented Generation
